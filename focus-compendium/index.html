FAQ

Common questions

What is Focus Compendium?

Focus Compendium is an Android focus timer that connects completed focus sessions with item discovery and long-term fantasy collection.

Is Focus Compendium a Pomodoro timer?

It can be used for Pomodoro-style focus sessions, but its defining feature is the collection system attached to completed focus time.

Do I need an account?

No. Focus Compendium does not require a Byeolgwawon account or login.

Is there a subscription?

No. Focus Compendium does not use a subscription model.

Does Focus Compendium show forced ads?

No. Focus Compendium does not insert mandatory interruption ads into the focus flow. It uses optional rewarded ads that you choose whether to watch.

Is Focus Compendium local-first?

Yes. Core focus records, settings, and collection progress are designed to stay on your device rather than in a Byeolgwawon account or remote progress database.

Can I use the timer without an internet connection?

Core focus and collection progress are local. Features that depend on Google services, such as rewarded ads or Google Play purchases, require network access.

Where can I read about data handling?

See the Privacy Policy for details about local data and third-party Google services.
